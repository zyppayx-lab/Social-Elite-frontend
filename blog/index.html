Why should I watch the tutorial before logging in?

Each tutorial explains the recommended login process and highlights important steps that can help you avoid common mistakes.

I can't log in after watching the tutorial.

Review the tutorial carefully to confirm every step was followed. If the issue continues, contact our support team and include your order number together with a description of the problem.
