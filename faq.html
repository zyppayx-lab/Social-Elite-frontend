Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to common questions.

1. What is SocialElite?

SocialElite is a digital marketplace where users can securely discover and purchase digital products available on the platform.

2. How do I create an account?

Select Sign Up, provide the required information, verify your email if requested and complete your profile.

3. What payment methods are accepted?

Available payment methods are displayed during checkout and may vary depending on your location and payment provider.

4. How will I receive my purchase?

After your payment has been successfully verified, eligible digital products will be delivered through your account dashboard or the applicable delivery method shown during checkout.

5. Can I request a refund?

Refund requests are reviewed individually according to our Refund Policy.

6. How can I contact support?

You can contact our support team using the contact information provided on the Contact Us page. Please include your registered email address and order number (if applicable) to help us assist you more quickly.

7. Is my personal information secure?

Yes. We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to help protect your personal information. For more details, please review our Privacy Policy.

8. Can I update my account information?

Yes. You can update your account information through your profile settings. Some changes may require additional verification for security purposes.

9. Why was my order cancelled?

Orders may be cancelled due to payment verification issues, suspected fraud, product unavailability or other circumstances necessary to protect our users and the Platform.

10. Can these policies change?

Yes. We may update our Terms of Service, Privacy Policy, Refund Policy and other policies from time to time. Updated versions will be published on the Platform.

11. I couldn't find my answer. What should I do?

If your question is not answered here, please contact our support team. We'll be happy to assist you as quickly as possible.
