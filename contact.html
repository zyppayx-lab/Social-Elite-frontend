Before Contacting Support

To help us resolve your issue quickly, please include:

  • Your registered email address.
  • Your order number (if applicable).
  • A detailed description of the issue.
  • Screenshots where applicable.

Reporting Security Issues

If you believe you have discovered a security vulnerability or suspicious activity on the Platform, please notify us immediately through our official support channels.

Please include sufficient details to help us investigate the issue. We appreciate responsible disclosure and will review all legitimate reports promptly.

Community Guidelines

To maintain a safe and reliable experience for everyone, we ask all users to communicate respectfully with our support team.

  • Be respectful and professional.
  • Provide accurate information about your issue.
  • Avoid abusive, threatening or offensive language.
  • Do not submit false reports or fraudulent claims.

Failure to comply with these guidelines may result in delayed assistance or restrictions on your account where appropriate.
